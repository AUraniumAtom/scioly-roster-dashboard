Scoring Engine Formulas

Percentile Rank

Percentile = 100 × (Count_Lower + 0.5 × (Count_Equal - 1)) / (Total_Candidates - 1)

Calculates candidate's relative standing within the division. Middle-rank correction applied for identical tied scores.

Normalized Score

Normalized = 100 × [w_p90 × min(1.05, Raw / P90) + w_pct × (Percentile / 100)]

For < 10 participants: w_p90 = 0.85, w_pct = 0.15. For ≥ 10 participants: w_p90 = 0.70, w_pct = 0.30.

Performance Index (Overall)

PI = (Tests ≥ 3) ? (0.7 × Average + 0.3 × Top-3) : Average

Emphasizes consistent multi-event strength while rewarding deep peak performance in top events.

Returning Tier Value Scale

S+: 8 | S: 7 | A+: 6 | A: 5 | B+: 4 | B: 3 | C: 2 | D: 1 | New: 0

Weights assigned to prior tournament placements and verified performance tiers.
